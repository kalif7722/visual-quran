# Theme guide: editorial references

Reviewed 6 October 2026. Themes are concise editorial study prompts, not exclusive classifications of entire Surahs. Each on-page passage links directly to Quran.com for translation, context and Tafsir. Reflection questions are editorial prompts rather than quotations.

## Coverage standard

All 20 theme cards now contain **10 curated Quran passages/examples each** (200 passage references total). The references are selected to provide broader coverage while keeping each passage clearly connected to the card’s learning theme.

The current theme set is:

- Faith & worship
- Mercy & hope
- Patience & resilience
- Gratitude & blessings
- Prayer & remembrance
- Character & community
- Family & kindness
- Justice & honesty
- Giving & service
- Creation & reflection
- Purpose & accountability
- Knowledge & wisdom
- Trust & reliance
- Forgiveness & healing
- Dua & seeking help
- Peace & reconciliation
- Moderation & avoiding waste
- Worldly life & lasting priorities
- Unity & good relations
- Sincerity & devotion

Verse bounds and Surah identifiers are validated against the site’s 114-Surah directory. Theme passages are study references rather than claims that a whole Surah belongs exclusively to one theme.
