# Theme guide: editorial references

Reviewed 6 October 2026. Themes are concise editorial study prompts, not exclusive classifications of entire Surahs. Each on-page passage links directly to Quran.com for translation, context and Tafsir. Reflection questions are editorial prompts rather than quotations.

## Additional themes

| Theme | Passages reviewed | Reason for inclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trust & reliance | [65:3](https://quran.com/65/3), [3:159](https://quran.com/3/159) | Reliance on Allah; consultation and decision before reliance. At-Talaq’s family-responsibility context is retained in the card. |
| Forgiveness & healing | [42:40](https://quran.com/42/40), [24:22](https://quran.com/24/22) | Pardon and reconciliation alongside justice; continuing generosity despite hurt. |
| Dua & seeking help | [2:186](https://quran.com/2/186), [21:87–88](https://quran.com/21/87-88) | Calling upon Allah and Yunus’s prayer in distress. |
| Peace & reconciliation | [49:10](https://quran.com/49/10), [8:61](https://quran.com/8/61) | Reconciliation among believers; responding to an enemy’s inclination to peace. These contexts are distinguished. |
| Moderation & avoiding waste | [7:31](https://quran.com/7/31), [6:141](https://quran.com/6/141) | Eating and drinking without waste; harvest dues and avoiding waste. |
| Worldly life & lasting priorities | [57:20](https://quran.com/57/20), [102:1–2](https://quran.com/102/1-2) | The passing attractions of worldly life and distraction through competition for more. |
| Unity & good relations | [3:103](https://quran.com/3/103), [60:8](https://quran.com/60/8) | Unity in faith and fairness toward those who are not hostile. |
| Sincerity & devotion | [98:5](https://quran.com/98/5), [39:2](https://quran.com/39/2) | Sincere worship, prayer and giving. |

## Expanded existing themes

- Character: [25:63](https://quran.com/25/63), humility and peaceful responses.
- Family and kindness: [4:36](https://quran.com/4/36), care for family, neighbours and people in need.
- Justice: [16:90](https://quran.com/16/90), justice, good conduct and generosity.
- Accountability: [59:18](https://quran.com/59/18), considering deeds sent forward.

Verse bounds and Surah identifiers are validated against the site’s 114-Surah directory. The site retains the original 12 themes with their existing verse references.
